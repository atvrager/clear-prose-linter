# Simplified Technical English (ASD-STE100) Reference Guide

ASD-STE100 is an international specification for the preparation of technical documentation in a controlled language.

## Mechanical Rules

### 1. Sentence Length
- Procedural sentences: maximum 20 words.
- Descriptive sentences: maximum 25 words.

### 2. Paragraph Length
- Maximum 6 sentences per paragraph.

### 3. Punctuation
- Do not use semicolons; write two sentences.
- Use colons only to introduce lists or examples.

### 4. Grammar and Voice
- Use active voice only.
- Do not use contractions (`don't`, `can't`, `it's`). Always spell out both words.
- Do not use Latin abbreviations (`e.g.`, `i.e.`, `etc.`).
  - Replace `e.g.` with `for example`.
  - Replace `i.e.` with `that is`.
  - Replace `etc.` with `and so on`.

### 5. Vocabulary
- Words must have only one approved meaning and one approved part of speech.
- Do not use slang, idioms, or colloquialisms.
- Use `can` for ability and possibility. Use `must` for duty and requirements.
- Never use unapproved modal verbs (`should`, `could`, `would`, `might`).
